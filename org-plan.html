Executive Summary

The plan in one page.

Right-sizing the division at $30M now and scaling through $100M inside the same 3-department model. The investment target is 15% of revenue across every phase.

Where we are now  ·  $20-30M

Right-sizing the build.

  • 3 departments with non-overlapping mandates: Student Success (Abby), Curriculum & IP (Eliot), Program Delivery (open).
  • 6 FT today scaling to ~21 FT plus a 30-person elastic coach pool by $30M.
  • Investment hits ~$3.95M (~13%) during the build phase, well inside the 15% target.

Scale inside the same 3 departments.

  • ~$13.21M (~13% of revenue) trending below the 15% target as AI leverage compounds.

They take Architect direction and turn it into the running cohort experience. One per core program (A2A, Superhuman, Uncommon Leaders), $80-110K each.
